Our story

SAMWO was established on 27 June 2019 in Karachi as a platform for educationists, health workers, coaches, volunteers and community leaders to work toward one goal: sustainable, dignified support for the people around them.

What began as one founder's commitment to community service has grown into an organisation working across education, health, sports, community development and women empowerment — built on the belief that a healthy, educated and empowered community is the foundation of a stronger Pakistan.

Every programme is carried out in partnership with government institutions, educational bodies, volunteers and socially responsible individuals, with a shared commitment to transparency, accountability and lawful practice.

Where we focus

Five connected pillars — because a healthy, educated, empowered and engaged community is built one programme at a time.

Education

Widening access to schooling and learning support for children in underserved neighbourhoods.

Health

Basic care access, awareness activities and support connecting families to medical services.

Sports & Youth

Grassroots talent identification, training access and pathways for young athletes across Sindh.

Community Dev.

Grassroots partnerships with local leaders that turn shared needs into lasting local projects.

Women Empowerment

Inclusion, skills and leadership opportunities that help women shape their own communities.

Founder & President

Muhammad Nisar Awan

An educationist, social welfare professional, NGO consultant and community development advocate, Muhammad Nisar Awan founded SAMWO to give dedicated professionals, volunteers and community leaders a shared platform for sustainable, meaningful social impact across Sindh.
